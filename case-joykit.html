JoyKit — Activity Finder for Kids

Final project: a platform that helps parents quickly find suitable classes and activities for their kids nearby.

View on Figma ↗

Challenge

Parents struggle to find suitable classes nearby and can't easily sign up for a trial lesson without phone calls or lengthy searching.

Process

Created a moodboard in purple and orange (energy and creativity), mapped usage scenarios and the customer journey, and designed a top filter by category, age, price, time, and location.

Solution

A landing page with a clear value proposition for parents, a "top filter" section with easy-to-scan activity cards, and a mobile version — from search to booking a trial lesson in just a few clicks.

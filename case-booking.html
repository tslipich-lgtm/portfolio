Booking.com — Filter Redesign

UX research and redesign of an overloaded filter search: from problem analysis and persona to a comparison with Airbnb and Agoda.

View on Figma ↗

Problem

Booking.com's filter search is overloaded and unstructured: filters overlap, their arrangement is inconsistent, and users lose time and trust in the search results.

Persona

Alina Novak, 25 — a marketing manager who travels frequently for both work and leisure. She values visual clarity and a fast search, but the cluttered filters slow her down.

Competitive Analysis

Airbnb uses a separate, clearly laid-out filter window with icons. Agoda displays active filters individually and lets users remove them one by one. Both patterns informed the final solution.

Solution

Grouped filters by category (accommodation type, amenities, booking conditions), added icons, and moved them into a separate window to keep the results page clean.
